# ⚡ SilverStack Sidekick

> **Your open-source AI best friend and goal-alignment partner.**

SilverStack Sidekick is an autonomous personal companion designed to understand your vision, keep you accountable, and act as a true sounding board for your life, projects, and ambitions. 

Unlike rigid task managers or generic chat assistants, Sidekick is built to feel like a real friend in your corner—helping you cut through noise, organize your thoughts, stay focused on what actually matters, and drive continuous personal growth.

---

## ✨ Features

* 🧠 **Deep Context & Goal Alignment:** Remembers your long-term ambitions, current projects, and core values.
* 💬 **Authentic Sounding Board:** Talk through complex decisions, debug life/work roadblocks, and brainstorm freely.
* 🎯 **Proactive Accountability:** Keeps you on track with gentle check-ins and progress reflection without feeling like a bossy manager.
* 🔒 **Privacy-First:** Your data and personal context stay entirely under your control.
* 🛠️ **Developer-Friendly:** Easily extendable architecture built for customization and local setup.
